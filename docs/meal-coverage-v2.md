# Meal Coverage v2

## Product decision

Kitchen Readiness no debe preguntar solamente cuánto stock existe. Debe traducir una intención explícita —**qué meals quiero poder comer y cuántas veces por semana**— en inventario objetivo y reposición.

Meal Coverage v2 complementa los mínimos e ideales manuales; no los reemplaza.

## New input

Each active meal can define:

`target_servings_per_week`

A target of `0` means the meal remains available in the Meal Engine but does not influence the weekly stock plan.

## Derived demand

For every product used by planned meals:

`weekly_demand(product) = Σ target_servings_per_week(meal) × quantity_per_serving(meal, product)`

Shared ingredients are aggregated once across all planned meals.

## Effective stock targets

Manual targets remain authoritative floors.

`effective_min = max(manual_min, weekly_demand × 3 / 7)`

`effective_ideal = max(manual_ideal, weekly_demand)`

The three-day buffer is the first explicit policy for Meal Coverage v2. It means Smart Restock starts paying attention before the complete weekly plan is exhausted while preserving any stronger manual minimum.

## Weekly readiness

For each planned ingredient:

`ingredient_plan_days = stock_on_hand / weekly_demand × 7`

The limiting ingredient determines the conservative plan coverage:

`meal_plan_coverage_days = min(7, min(ingredient_plan_days))`

`meal_plan_readiness_pct = min(100, meal_plan_coverage_days / 7 × 100)`

This is intentionally conservative and auditable. It answers: **how much of the planned week can the current stock support if the explicit meal targets are respected?**

## Smart Restock

An ingredient enters the Meal Coverage attention queue when:

`stock_on_hand <= effective_min`

Suggested purchase quantity:

`suggested = max(effective_ideal - stock_on_hand, 0)`

The existing item-level min/ideal logic still works for products that are not part of any planned meal.

## First real meal

Reference meal based on the first user-provided meal example:

### Pollo con lentejas, brócoli y zanahoria

Initial target: **4 servings / week**

Per serving:

- Pollo: 200 g
- Lentejas: 70 g
- Brócoli: 120 g
- Zanahoria: 80 g

Weekly demand generated by this one meal:

- Pollo: 800 g
- Lentejas: 280 g
- Brócoli: 480 g
- Zanahoria: 320 g

The demo deliberately starts with 720 g of chicken so the UI can demonstrate a real limiting ingredient and an 80 g gap against the weekly meal target.

## User surfaces

### Inicio
Shows normal Home Meal Coverage plus weekly Meal Plan readiness, plan days and limiting ingredient.

### Meals
Shows current servings available and the weekly target when configured.

### Personal Kitchen
Allows setting `objetivo/semana` for every meal and shows aggregated ingredient demand, gaps and the limiting ingredient.

### Smart Restock
Uses effective meal-driven targets in addition to manual min/ideal stock.

### Reporting
Adds weekly Meal Plan readiness and coverage days.

### Email consequence loop / daily digest
A meal plan can now trigger attention when its proportional coverage crosses the configured day threshold, even if a manually configured item minimum has not yet been crossed.

## Boundaries of v2

Meal targets are explicit user intent. The system does **not** silently infer desired weekly frequency from past behavior yet.

Future intelligence can compare targets with actual meal events and propose an adjustment, but any automatic change to desired meal frequency should remain reviewable by the user.

A later vertical slice can turn a meal photo into a proposed recipe template (`photo → ingredients → quantities → target frequency → human confirmation`) and then feed it into the same Meal Coverage engine.
